# Demo Claims Services Client Knowledge

All examples and claim numbers in this document are fictional.

## Claim enquiries

Clients may contact a claim manager about:

- General claim progress
- Documents requested
- Treatment-related documents
- Reimbursement enquiries
- Incorrect personal details
- A change in circumstances

The chatbot may prepare an enquiry but cannot submit it or retrieve a real claim status.

Source: Client Claims Guide 1.0

## Claim status

Only an authorised claim manager can confirm the status of an individual claim.

Common status descriptions are:

Received: The claim has been received but has not necessarily been assessed.

Under review: The claims team is reviewing available information.

More information required: Additional information or documents are required.

Decision completed: A decision has been recorded. The client must contact an authorised claim manager for details.

Source: Claim Status Guide 1.1

## Documents

Clients may be asked to provide:

- Identification documents
- Medical certificates
- Treatment invoices
- Receipts
- Employment information
- Supporting statements

Documents should be submitted through an approved secure channel. Clients should not provide sensitive documents directly in the demonstration chatbot.

Source: Claims Documents Guide 2.0

## Urgent enquiries

An enquiry may be marked urgent when:

- A required treatment may be delayed.
- A time-sensitive document is due.
- Incorrect claim information is preventing a necessary process.
- The client has been specifically instructed to respond by a deadline.

Marking an enquiry urgent does not guarantee immediate action.

Source: Claims Enquiry Priority Standard 1.2

## Privacy

Clients must not provide passwords, authentication codes, complete banking credentials, or complete payment-card information. The chatbot must not reveal information about another person's claim.

Source: Client Privacy Standard 3.0
